# Reviewing this manual

Thank you for reading the colleague-review edition, version **2026-09-17.1**.

Useful feedback includes:

- An anatomical or clinical claim that needs correction or a stronger source.
- An explanation that is unclear or too compressed to teach the topic.
- An important response type, study, or disagreement that is missing.
- Wording that could be mistaken for an approved protocol or a patient-level prediction.

Use **Review Chapter** at the end of the relevant chapter. The link opens a GitHub issue draft; it does not submit anything automatically. Include the chapter and heading, the sentence in question, your concern, proposed wording, and a reference if relevant. One focused issue per topic is easiest to discuss.

[Open a general review](https://github.com/erafat/cortical-stimulation-manual-review/issues/new?template=chapter-review.md) or [read existing comments](https://github.com/erafat/cortical-stimulation-manual-review/issues).

Feedback is public. Please omit patient-identifying information. If you do not use GitHub, send the same details directly to the colleague who shared the manual. Clinical signoff and adoption of local procedures remain separate from editorial review.
